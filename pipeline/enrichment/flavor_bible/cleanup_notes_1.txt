a lot of the pairings are not ingredients, but cuisine relationships, 
which will be valuable later, but needs to be pulled out of the ingredients tables.
to convert the "target" dim, there ", in" or "with" are specific pairings and can include sub-regions. 
the words "cuisine" "foods" and "dishes" are quick indicators that we're not talking about ingredients.

there are leading categories that need to be extracted carefully, since "vinegar, balsamic" is "balsamic vinegar" and 
is a subset of "vinegar", but "berries, strawberries" is not a "strawberry berries" even though it is a subset of "berries":
